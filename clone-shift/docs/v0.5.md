# Clone Shift v0.5

This implementation combines the approved v0.5 visual refinement with the newer gameplay decisions recovered from the November 1 project conversation. The current v0.4 source was the migration baseline. The older GDD v0.2 is historical context; the v0.5 decisions below supersede its different start state, eight-second retreat, three-stage structure and earlier UI direction.

## Approved experience

- Pixel-art ruined mall, moss and muted decay, warm brown/gold panels, chunky tactile buttons.
- Four persistent tabs: Home, Attack, Train, Upgrades. Persistent DPS, loot/sec, credits/sec and totals.
- All clone artwork uses one base image. Only role accessories differ: sword, shield, wizard hat, hood, monocle.
- Compact DPS strip: damage per second and attacker count. Physical/magic details remain on Home.
- Home retains workforce, clone buying, loot flow, combat output and squad health.
- Attack shows continuously animated combat, squad/enemy HP, stages, actual per-second enemies defeated, loot recovered, loot processed and credits converted. Scavenger and processor activity is animated.
- Train has compact Offensive, Defensive and Utility groups, assigned count, shared level, XP and +/- controls. Workforce and Training views separate the two types of assignment.
- Upgrades preserve the existing seven upgrade lines, grouped into combat and economy. Labels reflect their actual effects.
- Pressing buttons lowers them and compresses their shadows. Reduced animation respects the device preference and can be selected in settings.

## Exclusive assignments and test setup

Every clone has exactly one assignment. A trainer does not also fight, recover loot or process loot. Training XP is shared across all relevant workers, not individual characters.

## Single economy simulation

`engine.js` is the source for combat, training, offline progress and every displayed production rate:

1. Physical and magic output damage the current enemy continuously.
2. Defeating an enemy places its loot on the ground. No credits or loot are duplicated by damage ticks.
3. Scavenging workers recover ground loot into finite storage, up to recovery capacity and available space.
4. Processing workers consume stored loot, up to processing capacity, and produce credits.
5. Credits buy clone capacity with a clone, and the existing upgrade lines.

The top HUD and Attack flow use actual measured output over the recent five seconds. Enemy defeats/sec records completed defeats, not estimated DPS divided by HP. Processing/sec is actual consumption, with processing capacity shown separately. During initial startup the actual rates are zero until production occurs.

Ground loot is buffered up to twice the storage capacity. Excess ground loot is discarded and recorded. Full stored loot stops recovery; processing can free space. The simulation records recovered loot, processed loot, credits, completed kills and discarded loot independently for verification.

The v0.4 upgrade costs and effects are retained: physical/magic +25%, armor +24% HP, guard +8% block up to 72%, storage +18, processing +30%, scavenging +16% yield. Shared trainers earn 0.45 XP per assigned trainer per second, with 10 XP per level. Combat/arcane give +5% damage per level; conditioning +4% HP; scavenging/processing +5% throughput per level. Legacy role bonuses are retained during migration.

## Combat and recovery

- Seven regular enemies lead to an automatic boss. Boss defeat advances the arena.
- Arenas: Ruined Atrium, Abandoned Supermarket, Quarantine Hospital, Flooded Underground, Fallen City Center, The Black Gate.
- Zero squad HP starts a five-second team-wipe countdown. Combat and new enemy drops stop during recovery. Existing loot processing and shared training continue.
- Resources and clones are retained. The squad redeploys at full HP to the regular enemies of the current arena, so a boss cannot permanently trap the economy.
- The final arena repeats its boss cycle and increases the existing permanent Awakening damage bonus.
- Existing Overdrive behavior is retained: double DPS for eight seconds, twenty-second cooldown.

## Saves and delivery

Saves are browser-local at the existing GitHub Pages origin. `clone_shift_v05` migrates v0.4 and v0.3 saves, retaining credits, stored loot, upgrades, purchased-clone pricing and training. Missing protected assignments are filled; old saves receive the 20-clone test baseline without deleting earned progress. Offline progress is limited to twenty minutes and runs through the same simulation. Saves write every four seconds and when hiding or leaving the page.

No external account, analytics, paid service or runtime dependency is required. All runtime art and fonts ship with the game.

## Verification

Run `node clone-shift/tests/engine.test.cjs` and `node clone-shift/tests/art.test.cjs` from the repository root. The checks cover allocation, protected assignments, loot conservation, displayed rates, storage limits, trainer exclusivity, wipe recovery, migrations, purchases, offline simulation, arena progression and storage failures.

Mobile browser QA covers the four screens, both assignment views, buying clones/upgrades, preserved saves after reload and the absence of horizontal overflow at phone widths. Phone-sized browser QA is not a physical iOS-device test.

The canvas-state regression check ensures icons and repeated animation frames restore their transforms, preventing the processing station and credits from drifting off the production strip.
