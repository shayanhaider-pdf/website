    How to play

    1. Clones begin as Generalists. Assign them to one of eight jobs with the + buttons.

    2. Attack and Magic deal damage, while Defense helps the squad survive. Trainers grow shared stats.

    3. Hunting earns loot, Scavenging increases loot yield, and Processing converts it into Credits.

    4. Buy upgrades, clear three stages, challenge the Gate Guardian, and Awaken for a permanent damage bonus.
